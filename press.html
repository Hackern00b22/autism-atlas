How this learns
The 👍/👎 verdicts train the same smoothed log-odds overlay the research branches learn from — a release's title/summary terms stand in for an article's MeSH headings. Terms that recur among kept releases rank new arrivals up; terms that recur among rejected ones rank them down and dim strong mismatches. A term needs to appear in at least two verdicts before it is learned.
